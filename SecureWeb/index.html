Understand Your Cybersecurity Risk

Cyber threats can affect anyone. Understanding common risks is the first step toward better protection.

🎣 Phishing

Fake emails, messages and websites designed to steal sensitive information.

🔑 Weak Passwords

Simple or reused passwords can make accounts easier to compromise.

🦠 Malware

Malicious software can damage systems or compromise personal information.

📡 Unsafe Networks

Untrusted networks can expose sensitive information and increase security risks.
